For people who can’t afford a lawyer, even a routine legal problem can be hard to untangle. We’re planning an attorney-supervised clinic to help close that gap.

Our mission

Make everyday legal problems easier to face.

Our aim is to bring pro bono legal help to people who might otherwise face a housing notice, a debt claim or an unfamiliar court paper on their own. The long-term ambition is nationwide. Work would begin locally, only where appropriately licensed attorneys can supervise it.

The proposed model

How the clinic would work

A person should know who is responsible for their matter and what help they can expect.

  1. 1

    Start with a local need

    Community organizations could help identify unmet needs. The clinic would choose an initial service area and define which matters its volunteers could handle.

  2. 2

    Match the matter to a lawyer

    Before offering services, the clinic would check conflicts, confirm the attorney’s license for the jurisdiction and agree on the scope of help with the client.

  3. 3

    Keep a lawyer responsible

    Technology could help organize records or prepare a first draft. The supervising attorney would check the work, give the advice and remain accountable to the client.

Ways to get involved

Help shape
the clinic.

Building a clinic takes more than lawyers. We welcome interest from people who can help design it, govern it and understand the communities it hopes to serve.

Interest contact · Placeholder

A verified contact for these roles will appear here before outreach begins. Please do not send case details or documents.

Prospective board members

Help set priorities, steward resources and protect independent decision-making. Experience in community service, nonprofit finance or governance would be especially useful.

Volunteer attorneys

Help design manageable pro bono assignments and a sound supervision model. Future participation would depend on licensing, practice experience and time available.

Community nonprofits

Share where people struggle to find legal help and which barriers matter locally, from language access to transportation. Help shape workable referral options before any referrals begin.

Supporting firms

Law firms and technology firms, including competitors of Agentic Attorney, are welcome to explore contributions of expertise, training or volunteer time. Buying or using a particular platform would not be a condition of participation.

Governance & readiness

Before services begin

The clinic is in planning. Its organizers still need to establish its structure, seat a board and put safeguards in place. Incorporation and tax-exempt status are not claimed.

Independent oversight
Define who can approve spending, manage conflicts and evaluate any support agreement.
Client protection
Set rules for confidentiality, attorney review, insurance and secure records before opening intake.
Local readiness
Confirm attorney coverage, available volunteer time and referral options before offering help in a jurisdiction.

Proposed support

Where Agentic Attorney fits

Agentic Attorney could provide technology and operational support, such as document tools or volunteer scheduling. Any role would require agreed terms and clinic oversight. This is a proposal, with no executed support agreement represented here.

The proposed clinic is separate from AgenticAttorney.com, the commercial platform, and GoSueThem.org, the separate legal-cause funding initiative.
