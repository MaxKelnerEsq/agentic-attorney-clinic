The mission

Make the first legal question
easier to ask.

A letter about housing or debt can be hard to read alone. The proposed clinic would bring community organizations and volunteer attorneys together to offer pro bono help with everyday legal problems. Its ambition is nationwide. Services could begin only where attorneys are appropriately licensed and supervision is in place.

The proposed model

Follow one notice.

A rent notice lists an unpaid balance. The tenant says they have a receipt. Follow that question through three possible handoffs.

Step 2 / Preparation

Keep the original in view.

A volunteer could use document tools to prepare a summary. The notice and the tenant’s account would stay separate. Whether the receipt relates to the balance would remain an open question for counsel.

Step 3 / Attorney review

A lawyer decides what help is possible.

An attorney licensed in the relevant jurisdiction would check conflicts, review the original notice and ask about the receipt. If the clinic could help, the attorney would explain the scope to the tenant and supervise the work.

Who gives legal advice?The responsible attorney.

Participate

Who can help build it?

The proposal welcomes people from different professions, including firms that compete with Agentic Attorney.

Prospective board membersSet priorities

Help set the clinic’s priorities, oversee its resources and make independent decisions about outside support.

A first conversation How should a board weigh community need against the clinic’s capacity?

Volunteer attorneysSupervise legal work

Help design manageable pro bono assignments and a supervision model. Future work would depend on licensure, experience and availability.

A first conversation What scope of work could you responsibly take on?

Community nonprofitsShape referrals

Tell the organizers where people struggle to find legal help and how language, transportation or trust affects access. Help design a referral path before referrals begin.

A first conversation Where do people get stuck when they ask for help?

Supporting firmsOffer time or tools

Law and technology firms could contribute volunteer time, training or expertise. Participation would be open to competitors and would not require using a particular platform.

A first conversation What could your team contribute while leaving legal decisions to the supervising attorney?

Readiness

Before a first case.

The clinic is in planning. Incorporation, tax-exempt status and partnerships are not claimed.

Independent governance
Seat a board, assign oversight and decide how conflicts and spending would be reviewed.
Protection for clients
Establish confidentiality, attorney review, insurance and secure record handling before opening intake.
Jurisdiction by jurisdiction
Confirm attorney licenses, available supervision, capacity and referral options before offering local services.

Related initiatives

Where support could come from.

Agentic Attorney could provide technology and operational support, such as document tools or volunteer scheduling. Any role would need agreed terms and independent clinic oversight. No executed support agreement is represented here.

The proposed clinic is separate from AgenticAttorney.com, the commercial platform, and GoSueThem.org, the legal-cause funding initiative.
