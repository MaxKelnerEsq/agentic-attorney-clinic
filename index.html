The purpose

Make legal help
easier to reach.

The proposed clinic would connect people who cannot afford legal help with volunteer attorneys, through community organizations that understand local needs. The ambition is nationwide; services could begin only where appropriately licensed attorneys and supervision are in place.

Where you could
contribute.

The clinic needs people willing to help design it. These are proposed roles, open to different backgrounds and competing firms.

Prospective board membersSet priorities

Shape the clinic’s purpose, set priorities and oversee its resources. Help establish a board that can make independent decisions about funding and outside support.

Volunteer attorneysSupervise legal work

Design pro bono assignments that fit your experience and availability. Help establish how attorneys would supervise volunteers and review technology-assisted work. Future services would depend on appropriate licensure.

Community nonprofitsShape referrals

Bring knowledge of the people you serve: the legal problems they face, the languages they speak and the obstacles to getting help. Help design referrals before any begin.

Supporting firmsOffer time or tools

Contribute attorney time, training or technical expertise. Competitors of Agentic Attorney are welcome. Participation would not require adopting a particular platform.

What comes
before opening.

This is a proposal. Incorporation, tax-exempt status and partnerships are not claimed. The following work remains before services could begin.

Independent governance
Seat a board, assign oversight and decide how conflicts and spending would be reviewed.
Protection for clients
Establish confidentiality, attorney review, insurance and secure record handling before opening intake.
Jurisdiction by jurisdiction
Confirm attorney licenses, available supervision, capacity and referral options before offering local services.

Proposed support.
Independent oversight.

Agentic Attorney could provide technology and operational support, such as document tools or volunteer scheduling. Any role would require agreed terms and independent clinic oversight. No executed support agreement is claimed.

The proposed clinic is separate from AgenticAttorney.com, the commercial platform, and GoSueThem.org, the legal-cause funding initiative.
